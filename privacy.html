Privacy Policy

Application: Magnolia Finance Ops (internal QuickBooks integration)
Owner: Magnolia CFI Academy
Effective: October 29, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the internal application Magnolia CFI Academy uses to connect to its own QuickBooks Online company. The application is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

2. Data accessed

With the authorization of a Magnolia CFI Academy QuickBooks administrator, the application reads Magnolia CFI Academy's accounting records, such as accounts, customers, invoices, payments, deposits and reports. It accesses no other company's data.

3. Why it is used

Solely for Magnolia CFI Academy's internal bookkeeping, financial reporting and reconciliation with its own scheduling and payment-processing records.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. It is used only by authorized Magnolia CFI Academy personnel and the service providers that host and operate the application on its behalf.

5. Security and retention

Access credentials are stored as protected secrets and are never published. Working copies of data are temporary and deleted after each task. Accounting records remain in QuickBooks Online.

6. Revoking access

A QuickBooks administrator can disconnect the application at any time from QuickBooks Online under Settings → Apps (Connected apps).
